Maker's corner

I'm Karina, a tech professor at a business school in Paris. I explore what 3D printing can do beyond solid objects: fabric-like pieces, handwriting, delicate patterns. I build my own design tools with Claude and write custom G-code to get there. This page gathers what I share with other makers: free printables and booklets on how the tools are made.

Maker's booklets

The behind-the-scenes of one design at a time: the idea, how I work with Claude, the tool I built, and the print settings. Method, not files, so you can apply it to your own work.

Booklet #1 · PDF

Notebook doodles: handwriting and doodles

How the "Kid's notebook" design was made, from the first idea to the finished piece. Inside: where the idea came from, how I work with Claude (practical advice and the real prompts I used), the web tool that generates the design, how the handwriting and doodling are implemented, how the background is built, printer settings and filaments, and scans of the finished pieces.

Useful whether you print your own work or just want to see what building with an AI assistant looks like on a real project. Digital PDF, designed for screen. No ready-made print files included.
