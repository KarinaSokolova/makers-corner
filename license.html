Who it's for

Individuals and small businesses who make and sell their own prints: independent makers, craft-fair sellers and small online shops.

Large businesses, retail chains, wholesalers and high-volume manufacturers are not covered. If that's you, write to me on Instagram for a separate licence. The same goes for wholesale: selling prints to other shops or resellers needs a separate agreement.

Each licence is for one person and one business. It can't be shared or transferred.

Which designs are covered

The licence covers all my original designs published on MakerWorld, existing and future, for as long as your membership is active, plus premium files bought directly from me when available.

Not covered: remixes, derivative works, third-party designs, and any model I mark as excluded from commercial use. Selling a remix of another creator's design would also need that creator's permission, which this licence can't give.

What's allowed

You may

  • Sell physical 3D prints of the covered designs, online or in person, on any platform
  • Choose your own colours and materials, and scale the designs
  • Print as many pieces as your small business needs
  • Sell under your own shop name, with the credit line below

You may not

  • Share, sell or upload the files (3MF, G-code, STL…), original or modified
  • Claim the designs as your own
  • Change the shape of a design without my written permission
  • Make the designs by moulding, casting or mass production
  • Sell prints wholesale (to other shops or resellers) without a separate agreement with me
  • Use my photos, videos or descriptions in your listings

Files and printing

The licence gives you the right to sell prints. It does not include the files themselves, printing support or any guarantee about print results.

The files are shared as they are on MakerWorld, for free. Many of my designs are technically demanding: several colours, very thin layers and custom settings that may not work the same on every printer or filament. Getting good results is your responsibility.

Please download and print a test before you subscribe, to check that the designs work on your printer. Membership fees are not refunded because a design could not be printed.

Cancellation

The licence is valid only while your membership is active and paid. When it ends, the right to sell ends the same day: stop selling prints of my designs, including stock already printed, and remove those listings.

You can still print the files for yourself, as the platform where you got them allows.

Questions

Where do I get the files?

The free designs are on my MakerWorld profile. Premium files, when available, are sold separately in my shop. The licence covers the right to sell prints, not the files themselves.

Is there a limit on how many prints I can sell?

No, as long as you are an individual or small business and your membership is active.

What if a design doesn't print well on my printer?

The files are provided as they are, and the licence doesn't include printing help. That's why I ask you to print a test before subscribing. You're welcome to share your results in the comments on MakerWorld, where other makers often help each other.

Who is responsible for the products I sell?

You are. As the maker and seller, you handle the quality and safety of your prints and the rules where you sell (product safety, labelling, taxes).

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your Stripe receipt to manage or cancel your subscription, or write to me on Instagram.
